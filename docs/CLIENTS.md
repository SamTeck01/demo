# The twenty clients

How they were chosen: all 2,100 rows of the Lead Tracker were checked. 1,636 unique websites were fetched, about 1,090 loaded, and roughly 500 independent agency sites were screenshotted and judged by eye. Franchise offices were left out. Each finalist was checked as a real business on Zillow, Homes.com, HAR, Property Finder or in the press, and contact details were taken from the agency's own website where possible. Many emails and phones in the original sheet were wrong; the corrected ones are in each client's JSON (`email`, `phone`) and in the private shortlist file kept outside this repo.

Each site carries an idea taken from the client itself, so no two look templated. The opening line is the problem a client can see in five seconds on their current site; lead with it.
