# Austin Boutique Realty — plan

**Client:** Drew Buchanan, broker, 2507 Janice Drive, Austin 78703. Phone 512 228 1579, austinboutiquerealty@gmail.com. He takes a handful of homes at a time in Tarrytown, Rollingwood and central Austin and sells each one himself.
**Who pays him:** sellers of $1–5M homes who want one senior person, not a team (listing commission).
**Current site:** still shows the template email user@domain.com.
**Dribbble reference:** "Elyse Residence – Luxury Real Estate Website Design" (dribbble.com/shots/25815603). It has:
- near-black rounded panels in a bento grid
- a giant white serif name over a photo of a modern house under a tree
- italic uppercase serif subheads
- one very large numeral for a figure

## Idea
A shop window. Few homes, each shown huge. The tool here is the **launch plan**: a seller ticks what the home needs and sees the week-by-week plan to market that Drew runs himself.

## Home, top to bottom
1. **Nav:** the wordmark, a "Book a visit" pill and a menu.
2. **Hero:** in a rounded dark frame, BOUTIQUE in giant serif at the bottom left, with an italic "Fewer homes. Sold personally." and the supporting copy on the right.
3. **Featured home:** a bento of a large image, the square footage as one large figure, then the facts and the button.
4. **On the market:** the other homes in bento tiles of mixed sizes.
5. **Launch plan:** checkboxes for prep, staging, photography and film, twilight shoot and a coming-soon preview. The timeline of weeks to market updates. Then "Ask Drew for this plan".
6. **Drew:** monogram and a statement in his own words.
7. **Contact:** phone, address, hours and a form.
8. **Footer:** full-width wordmark and IABS links.
9. **Phone bar:** Call Drew, Email and "Sell with Drew".
