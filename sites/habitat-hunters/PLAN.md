# Habitat Hunters — plan

**Client:** Habitat Hunters, an independent Austin brokerage with eleven agents. It has worked from 1804 W Koenig Lane since 1972. Phone 512 482 8651, info@habitathunters.com.

**Who pays them:**
- Sellers in central and north Austin (listing commission), the main money.
- Buyers who choose a neighbourhood first and a house second.

**Their current site:** the homepage hero is an empty black box.

**Dribbble reference:** "Harvasta — Luxury Real Estate Landing Page" (dribbble.com/shots/27533848). It has:
- a full-width landscape photo with the name set in giant cream serif capitals across its foot
- pill navigation over the sky
- a cream page
- two-column "About" copy in warm brown

## Idea

Their name is the device. HABITAT runs across the hero like the Harvasta wordmark. The site then reads like a naturalist's field guide: every neighbourhood is an entry, and every home is filed under its habitat.

## Sitemap

- `/habitat-hunters/`: home
- `/habitat-hunters/home/:id`: one page per home, with gallery, facts, monthly cost and a showing request
- `/habitat-hunters/saved`: the homes saved with the heart

## Home, top to bottom

1. **Nav:** a round house logo with "Habitat Hunters / since 1972", pills for Homes, Sell, Field guide and Payments, and a Contact button. On phones: the logo and a Menu sheet.
2. **Hero:** the photo of a house under a live oak, with HABITAT in giant serif across its foot.
3. **About:** two columns. On the left: the Koenig Lane office, the year 1972 and eleven agents. On the right: the statement and two actions, find a home and price my home.
4. **Field guide:** a tab for each of five neighbourhoods. Each entry has its photo, character, median price, typical homes, schools and commute, plus a link to the homes there.
5. **Homes:** filter chips by neighbourhood and by maximum price, and cards with a heart.
6. **Sell:** a value range from neighbourhood, size and condition, then "send me a full valuation".
7. **Payments:** price, down payment and rate, with the Travis County tax added and a stacked bar showing loan, tax and insurance.
8. **How a hunt goes:** four steps.
9. **Visit the office:** the address, hours, phone and a showing form.
10. **Footer:** HABITAT in full width.
11. **Phone bar:** Call, Email and "Price my home".
