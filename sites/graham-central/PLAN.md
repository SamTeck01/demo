# Graham Central Properties — plan

**Client:** Graham Central Properties, at 6002 Lonesome Valley Trail, Austin 78731. Phone 512 452 2000, kyle@grahamcentralproperties.com. They sell and lease homes in Tarrytown, Bryker Woods, Northwest Hills and the Hill Country.
**Who pays them:** sellers, through listing commission, and owners who lease their home through the firm, which brings recurring fees. Buyers and tenants come second.
**Their current site:** the listings show raw file names as captions.
**Dribbble reference:** "Luxury Real Estate Agency Website UI Design" (dribbble.com/shots/27108491), the "Rent Area" shot. It has a black-and-white building cut out against a solid red field, the name in giant black sans, a small uppercase menu across the red, a black search bar with four fields under the hero, and a giant "ABOUT" set beside a photo.
**Brand:** their own red-and-yellow G. Yellow is the action colour.

## Idea
They are the one firm that both sells and leases, so the live tool is the **Sell or lease?** comparison. An owner enters the home's value, what is left on the mortgage and the rent it would fetch. The tool shows the cash from selling today against a year of letting, net of the firm's management fee.

## Sitemap
- `/graham-central/` is the home page.
- `/graham-central/home/:id` is the page for each home.
