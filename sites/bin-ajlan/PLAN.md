# Bin Ajlan Properties — plan

**Client:** Bin Ajlan Properties is a licensed Qatari brokerage, founded in 2008. Its office is on Salwa Road, in the Industrial Area of Doha. Contact: +974 4414 1464, info@binajlan.net. It sells, leases, manages and values warehouses, showrooms, shops, labour accommodation and villas.

**Who pays them:**
- Businesses leasing commercial space, which brings in volume.
- Owners of commercial assets, who pay for sales and management.
- Banks and families who commission licensed valuations.

## Idea
Most of their customers are businesses, not families. The working tool is a **space finder**:
- The visitor picks the type of space, the size and the area.
- It shows the likely monthly rent at local rates per square metre.
- It shows the matching spaces in stock.
- One button asks for a shortlist.
