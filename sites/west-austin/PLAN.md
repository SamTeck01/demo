# West Austin Properties — plan

**Client:** Laura Duggan has sold homes west of MoPac since 1979, and her daughter Katy sells beside her. Phone 512 750 2425, Laura@WestAustin.com. They cover Tarrytown, Westlake, Rollingwood, Northwest Hills and Bryker Woods.

**Who pays them:** owners of $1–3M homes who want to sell discreetly (listing commission). Second come buyers relocating into the Eanes and Casis school zones.

## Idea

Selling a $2M home is about how public you go. The site's working tool is the **Ways to sell**: fully marketed, coming soon, or private. Each choice shows what happens, who sees the home and how long it takes. The site then asks for a private valuation.

## Home page, top to bottom

1. **Nav:** Menu on the left, WEST AUSTIN centred, and "Call Laura" plus a square arrow on the right.
2. **Hero:** a warm photograph of a house at dusk, with the headline "West of MoPac, since 1979". Below it: "View current homes" and "Book a private tour".
3. **Current homes:** a large editorial list that alternates the image left and right, with neighbourhood filter chips.
4. **Ways to sell:** three options, each with what happens, who sees the home and a typical timeline, followed by the private valuation form.
5. **The family:** Laura and Katy, with monogram portraits rather than invented photos, and the few facts that are real.
6. **Neighbourhoods:** five rows, each with the median price, the schools and the character of the area.
7. **Contact:** hours, phone, email and a private tour form.
8. **Footer:** the full-width wordmark and the Texas IABS links.
9. **Phone bar:** Call Laura, Email, and Private valuation.
