## Idea
"Our gems" are three buildings, presented the way the reference presents one residence.
- **Hero:** the left panel is a building picker. Arrows cycle the three buildings and change the spec table, the drawn floor plan and the photograph.
- **Move-in calculator:** pick a unit and the number of cheques. It shows the cash needed on signing: first cheque, security deposit and the Kahramaa deposit. There is no agency fee, because the lease is with the owner.
