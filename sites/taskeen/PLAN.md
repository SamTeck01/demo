# Taskeen Al Oula — plan

**Client:** Taskeen Al Oula Real Estate, Kuwait City, part of 3s International since 2021. They manage residential buildings for their owners: rent collection through an app, 24-hour maintenance, leasing and monthly reports. Contact: +965 1822 022, marketing@taskeen1re.com.

**Who pays them:** building owners, through recurring management fees. Tenants are secondary: they fill the empty flats.

**Their current site:** the homepage is only their logo.

**Dribbble reference:** the "Urelle" landing page. It has:
- a white page with Menu on the left, a centred sun mark, and an "Enquiries" link on the right
- a photograph in a frame, with a light sans headline at the bottom left and a round arrow button
- "{About}" with an image and a narrow text column
- a quiet contact form
- an orange footer with the name in giant white type

## Idea
The owner's quiet month. The first screen promises "Your building, managed." The proof is an **owner app preview**, a real HTML fragment showing what an owner sees: rent collected this month, open repairs and empty flats. Beside it sits the **management calculator**: flats, rent and current vacancy go in, and what Taskeen adds to the owner's year comes out.
