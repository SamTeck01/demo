## Idea
Buying property in Qatar can come with residency. The tool is the **residency checker**: move the price and it shows what the purchase unlocks. QAR 730,000 or more gives a residence permit for the owner and family. QAR 3,650,000 or more gives the permanent-residency benefits for property owners. It then lists homes at that threshold.
