# Ocean Club Realty — plan

**Client:** Ocean Club Realty is the independent office inside the Ocean Club, on Crandon Boulevard in Key Biscayne. Phone 305 361 2020, admin@oceanclubrealtyinc.com.
**Who pays them:**
- Sales in Ocean Tower One, Club Tower, Lake Tower and the lakefront villas.
- Owners who let their units for the season, which brings recurring fees.
**Their current site:** the logo sits on top of the menu on the homepage.
**Dribbble reference:** "Modern Architecture Real Estate Website Design" (dribbble.com/shots/27151302), "The Infinite Precision of Living". Its elements:
- a dusk-blue photograph with a condensed bold uppercase headline
- a black panel at the bottom left carrying one huge figure and prev/next arrows
- a small stacked nav and a white "Book a Viewing" pill

## Idea
"We are steps away." Every residence is measured in steps to the sand. The hero's black panel is a **tower picker**: the arrows cycle Ocean Tower One, Club Tower, Lake Tower and the villas, and each shows its steps to the sand in huge type, what it faces and its starting price.
The second live tool is the **season planner** for owners. Tick the months to let, choose the tower, and see the season's income after the management fee.
