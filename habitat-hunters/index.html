How a hunt goes

  1. We listen first

    One conversation about how you live: the commute, the school, the yard you want or don't.

  2. We scout ahead

    Our agents see homes before they list, so you hear about the right one early.

  3. We negotiate in-house

    Jeff Plotkin, our co-owner, is an attorney and CPA. Contracts are read by someone who knows the law.

  4. We stay after closing

    Fifty years in, many of our clients are second-generation. We'd like you to be one.
